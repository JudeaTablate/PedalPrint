Proposal Overview

Title: PedalPrint: A Kinetic Chain Fusion System Pairing Taiwan's Crank and Insole Engineering for Predictive Cycling Injury Prevention

Target Submission: Go Healthy with Taiwan 2026

Core Innovation: Cross-sensor phase correlation combining pedal spindle torque dynamics and vector smart insoles to detect compensation patterns prior to injury emergence.

Target Partners: Giant Manufacturing, Merida, Dr. Foot, KMC Chain, Sun Race Sturmey-Archer, WiseChip, and CHC (Cycling Common Protocol Alliance).
